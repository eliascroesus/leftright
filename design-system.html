What to check

Everything on this page is built from the real site files (css/style.css, js/rig.js, js/fx.js, js/pfp.js). The poses here are frozen; index.html is where they move.

  1. LEFTY and RIGHTY are one build, mirrored: same body, face and size. Only colour, hat and hair differ (she has bangs, lashes and a ponytail).
  2. Neither reads as smarter, stronger or the joke. The beanie + pom-pom and the cap + ponytail carry the same visual weight.
  3. Every pose reads at a glance, and fists stay visible in all of them.
  4. The red and blue halves feel equally heavy. Cream text stays readable on both.
  5. The paper grain sits on the flat backgrounds only (never on the characters or text), with no gradients anywhere.
  6. Hover the page: your cursor is the fist (neutral until a side is picked).

The fighters

One SVG rig in a flat cutout-cartoon style, drawn facing right. RIGHTY is the same rig mirrored and recoloured, with the gal hair set. Wide round heads, big touching eyes, mittens, tiny legs, one brow higher than the other, temple veins, the same forward lean. Funny and pathetic, never menacing. Swap who wears the hair in TEAMS in js/rig.js.

Lefty

Red beanie with a pom-pom, red coat, permanently mid-shout. Always on the left.

Righty

Blue cap, ponytail, blue coat, permanently mid-shout. Always on the right.

Every pose

Poses are plain data (LR.rig.POSES in js/rig.js): partial states merged over the base. The idle loop, the fight and the easter egg all animate between them.

Rage build

The longer the page is open, the redder the face and the bigger the veins. Every 30 seconds it resets with a puff of steam from both ears. Only opacity and scale change, so it stays at 60fps.

The rig

Parts carry their transform origin in the markup (data-pivot="x y", viewBox units), so rotations stay correct when the rig is mirrored. The yellow pins mark each origin on the base pose.

PartOriginMoves

Colour tokens

Defined once on :root in css/style.css and read live here. The two backgrounds share the same OKLCH lightness and chroma, so neither half is heavier. --accent is referee yellow until a side is picked, then the whole page leans red or blue.

Type

Bowlby One for everything shouted, Inter for everything read. Both load from Google Fonts, and both were checked for every glyph the copy uses. Neither font has a ★, so every star on the site is drawn as SVG.
